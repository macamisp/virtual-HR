## 5. Meeting participation evidence: effects on attendance

When the browser reports a call, the server counts it as meeting time only if it can see someone
else in it:

| Call | Evidence | Fails when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Office room | Another avatar in the same closed room (office registry) | The person is alone in the room |
| Proximity | Server-computed group of 2+ | The others walk away |
| Meeting | LiveKit lists this person and another participant (cached 30 s per room) | Alone in the meeting; meeting ended; LiveKit unreachable for > 90 s |

Effects:
- **Verified call:** `IN_MEETING`, no input needed, never idle; net = active + meeting, and input
  during the call is not added a second time (D1).
- **Unverified call:** ordinary input-based tracking; a silent person alone idles after 5 min and
  pauses after 15 min, as anyone else (D2). That time is lost on purpose: "being in an empty room"
  is not work evidence.
- **Second device / two tabs:** only the device in the call ends it (or its silence for 2
  intervals); tabs share the call state, so the leader tab reports a call owned by another tab.
- **Known edge effects:** (a) an avatar stepping out of the room for a moment ends and re-opens
  the meeting segment (the gap is ordinary time); (b) room evidence is co-presence, not LiveKit
  membership: a colleague in the room with media off still counts; (c) a LiveKit API outage stops
  scheduled-meeting credit after 90 s (office and proximity calls are unaffected); (d) the office
  registry is per process: heartbeats and office sockets must share it (true for the single-EC2
  stack and for the ECS realtime split; see `docs/verification/single-instance-assumptions.md`).

## 6. D1/D2 and historical records

- **The nightly replay never rewrites** (ADR 029: it alerts HR and audits). Since `b54b51e` the new
  meeting rules are gated by `meetingAccounting = 2`, which the live service stores with every
  input. Inputs recorded under V2 replay with the V2 rules, so the first replay after the release
  cannot raise false mismatches and no day is re-counted (tests: state machine "inputs recorded
  before V3 replay with the V2 rules", replay of the same history under both configs,
  meeting-time-e2e stored config).
- **Migrations change no existing value.** They add columns and tables and fill only the new
  columns: `timesheet_days/timesheets.meeting_minutes` from the existing `meeting_seconds` (display
  only), and `time_entries.task_title/project_name` from the current names. Amounts, hours,
  statuses, approvals and attendance rows are untouched; approved timesheets keep their stored
  payable amounts. `attendance_daily` is not recomputed.
- **Consequence:** days before the release keep the V2 numbers (including any D1 double count and
  D2 solo meeting time). From the release on, new heartbeats follow V3. A session open across the
  deploy switches rules at the restart. Release notes must say this.
