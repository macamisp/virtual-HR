## Limitations (not blocking)

- Reload on a non-office page ends the call (by ADR 048); Office rejoins.
- `inCall` comes from the heartbeat leader tab; if another tab of the same browser owns the call, it is not sent.
- `/documents` and `/timesheets` overflow on a phone (V1, P3).
