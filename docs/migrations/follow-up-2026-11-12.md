## Persistence, loss risks and independent backup recommendation

Docker named volumes can survive container replacement but do not by themselves establish
survival of EC2 termination or underlying disk loss. Actual storage-to-EBS mapping and each
attachment's DeleteOnTermination flag remain unknown. An unverified local pg_dump or EBS
snapshot cannot be treated as an independently recoverable, application-consistent backup.

Proposed strategy, subject to separate approval before infrastructure/IAM/KMS changes:

1. Preserve the current PostgreSQL volume and take a new consistent, verified encrypted backup
   once source access and an approved encrypted artifact location exist. Include application
   schemas/data, sequences, migrations, roles/grants and function/RLS ownership metadata.
2. Schedule encrypted logical exports to storage independent of this instance, with separate
   recovery access and checksums. Retain daily backups for 35 days, weekly for 12 months,
   with a cross-region copy per SRS 7.5. Restrict access, audit retrieval and monitor failures.
3. Daily dumps alone cannot meet the 5-minute RPO. Assess continuous WAL archiving/base
   backups on the source or managed PITR on the eventual destination. Any source setting,
   storage, role or policy changes require a separate reviewed implementation.
4. Run isolated restore drills quarterly; demonstrate RPO <=5 minutes and RTO <=2 hours.
   Retain independent exports even if provider-managed PITR is selected. Verify recovery of
   SeaweedFS objects and required KMS key access separately: a DB dump does not contain files
   or replace encryption keys. Do not copy production objects or invoke KMS merely to test this
   proposal without the required resource access approval.
